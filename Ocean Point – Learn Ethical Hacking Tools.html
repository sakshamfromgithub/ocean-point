Ground rules

    
Ethical hacking means testing with permission. These rules apply to every lesson on Ocean Point.

  

  
    
    
  • Practice only on your own lab, a virtual machine, or a platform built for training.
    • 
    
  • Get written permission before testing any system you do not own.
    • 
    
  • Report what you find. Do not keep, share or misuse data you come across.
    • 
    
  • Knowing the law in your country is part of the skill.
